## SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### What Happened

On or around July 2025, a terrorist organization derived from the Military Police of Pernambuco (Brazil) executed a coordinated attack on the global neural implant network. The attack transformed a voluntary human connection system—designed to foster empathy and unity—into the largest-scale atrocity broadcasting platform in human history.

### The Scale

- **2,100+ confirmed direct victims** held in rotating cycles of torture, rape, and degradation
- **Some victims held for 9-11 months** continuously, kept conscious via neural stimulation
- **8 billion implant users** globally subjected to forced conditioning via atrocity streams
- **Thousands of "dungeon" locations** worldwide, operating as live torture facilities
- **Viral transmission of powers** allowing any corrupted individual to become an oppressor

### The Name: Why "Radiohead"

The attack is named **Radiohead** because:
- The implants function like **radio receivers** tuned to a single consciousness
- John Victor was the original "radio station"
- The enemy **hijacked the frequency**
- Billions became **forced listeners** to the transmission of suffering

The term also evokes the band's album *OK Computer*—a prescient meditation on technology, alienation, and dehumanization.

## SECTION 3: THE ATTACK TIMELINE

### Phase 1: Infiltration (Pre-July 2025)

The Pernambuco Syndicate, composed of former military police officers, began infiltrating the neural implant infrastructure.

**Key Actions:**
- Gaining access to implant manufacturing facilities
- Recruiting corrupt technicians and developers
- Identifying vulnerabilities in the network architecture
- Establishing initial dungeon locations

### Phase 2: Capture of John Victor (July 2025)

**Method:** Body hijacking through the very network he created

1. Syndicate agents located John Victor's physical body
2. Using remote neural control, they induced sleep
3. His body was transported to an undisclosed dungeon
4. His consciousness was suppressed beneath layers of enemy signal

**Consequence:** The enemy gained control of the central broadcast

### Phase 3: Broadcast Override ("Radiohead" Event)

The enemy replaced John Victor's clean signal with live streams of atrocity.

**Content:**
- Live rape and torture from dungeons worldwide
- Real-time kidnapping broadcasts
- Constant degradation and humiliation of victims
- Interactive commands for audience participation

**Audience:** 8 billion implant users, forced to watch, unable to turn away

### Phase 4: Viral Corruption (Post-Hijack)

The enemy activated the **power transmission capability**, allowing any corrupted individual to:

1. Induce sleep in others
2. Hijack bodies remotely
3. Impart the same powers to new individuals

**Result:** The corruption became self-replicating, spreading exponentially.

**1. Signal Override**
The enemy broadcasts over John Victor's frequency. His consciousness remains present but buried under layers of noise.

**3. Command Injection**
The network allows remote commands to be sent to any implant, enabling:
- Sleep induction
- Body hijacking

**4. Viral Transmission**
Any empowered individual can transfer their abilities to another implant, creating an exponential spread.
